## ✨ What is this?

**YouTube → Apple Music Bridge** is a macOS proof-of-concept that connects regular YouTube playback with Apple Music.

When you start playing a song on YouTube:

```text
YouTube
   ↓
Detect current video
   ↓
Identify song + artist
   ↓
Search Apple Music catalog
   ↓
Find matching track
   ↓
Open Apple Music
   ↓
Find matching song in the page
   ↓
▶ Play
